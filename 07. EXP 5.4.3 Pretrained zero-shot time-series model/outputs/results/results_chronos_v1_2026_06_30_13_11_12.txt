Chronos v1 Architecture Notes
--------------------------------------------------
  Chronos v1 is a GENERATIVE autoregressive model (T5-based).
  It generates num_samples stochastic trajectories by sampling
  from a categorical distribution over bins at each step.
  Quantiles are extracted from the empirical sample distribution.
  The PI width reflects the model's true predictive uncertainty.

Protocol: sliding window, 1-step-ahead from 64-step forecast
  Context = 24 points (matches window size of all other models).
  Predict 64 steps, take 1st prediction only.
  Slide by 1 step, repeat for all test points.
  sigma2_model = 0 (zero-shot, no training randomness).
