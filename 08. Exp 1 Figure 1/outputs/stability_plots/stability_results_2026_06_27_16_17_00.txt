sigma2_model: mean((f_q^j - mean_q)^2) over runs x test points, summed over lo+hi heads.
sigma_model:  sqrt(sigma2_model) — in original y units.
SumofRMSEs:  (RMSE(mean_lo, true_lo) + RMSE(mean_hi, true_hi)) / 2 on the 600 test points (matches exp8 protocol).
PICP: fraction of test points covered by the mean PI.
MPIW: mean width of the mean PI on the test set.
IS: Interval Score — proper scoring rule (lower is better). IS = (u-l) + (2/alpha)*max(l-y,0) + (2/alpha)*max(y-u,0).
